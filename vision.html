01 / A precise definition

A digital twin is not a black-box score.

Metria represents a person as a versioned, longitudinal evidence model. It links source observations, clinically defined constructs, uncertainty, consent, model outputs, human corrections and final dispositions in one inspectable record.

“The evidence packet is the system’s true output. A risk score is only one field within it.”
02 / The decision loop

From evidence
to responsible action.

Every layer answers a practical question and keeps the path from signal to recommendation visible.

01

Observe

Capture speech, movement, clinical history, routines and patient-reported context with source and timestamp.

02

Verify

Estimate reliability, identify missing modalities, reconcile contradictions and abstain when evidence is insufficient.

Can we trust it?
03

What is happening now?
04

03 / What we will not compromise

Intelligence should make
judgement stronger.

Evidence before confidence

No claim without a traceable evidence packet, source quality and a clear account of what is missing.

Uncertainty is information

The system can defer, ask for more information or stop use. Abstention is a designed outcome, not a failure.

Humans remain in the loop

Clinicians and patients can inspect, correct, confirm, override or defer a recommendation with a recorded reason.

Models must learn responsibly

Versioning, drift monitoring, fairness checks, privacy, consent and incident learning remain part of the product.
